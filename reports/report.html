4. Real-world use

  • Risk bands: High (<10), Medium (10-13), Low (≥13) predicted grade.
  • Uncertainty: each prediction carries a 90% interval from held-out residuals.
  • No leakage: imputation, scaling and encoding are fitted inside the Pipeline on training data only.
  • Ethics: use predictions to offer support, never to penalise; audit for bias before deployment.
